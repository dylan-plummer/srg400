Exact count of the graphs obtained from this cover
24 November 2026

Allow an arbitrary order and sign of the seven characters at each of
the 50 fibers of the three-starter construction. The exact number of
unlabeled graphs is

 [ 2^215 * 15^50
   + 5  * 2^121 * 15^20 * 61^2
   + 8  * 2^39  * 15^10
   + 16 * 2^43  * 15^10
   + 20 * 2^25  * 15^4 ] / 50

 = 67149381404684988745612401176186472091382586932131677973141398197905438591233930267112149509803523803769257486605877248000.

The count is for a single unsigned tile decomposition. The proof in
README.txt shows that the graph determines that decomposition. This makes
the following enumeration an isomorphism count for ordinary graphs.

Local choices and their equivalences

To a fiber associate its eight binary sign words of length seven, in
the order of its incident tiles. These eight words form an affine coset
of a binary [7,3,4] simplex code. Permuting the character labels gives
30 such linear codes on the labeled set of seven ports. Each linear
code has 16 affine cosets. Thus there are 480 local states after
forgetting the order of the eight vertices inside a fiber.

The residual ambiguity over the identity map on the base is the group
K of 2^35 independent tile reversals. A reversal changes the signs at
all ten points of its tile. It preserves the product at each edge. To
see that these are all the equivalences, use the seven intrinsic splits
at each fiber as in README.txt. A relabeling identifying two local
sign-word sets can reverse whole ports. To preserve a block, the
reversal bits at its ends must agree. Each tile is connected, hence the
bits must be constant on that tile. Conversely these operations always
give isomorphic graphs.

K acts freely on the tuples of local states, including ones with
arbitrary character assignments. For a set of reversed tiles to fix
a fiber, their incidence vector at the fiber must belong to its linear
simplex code. Its weight must be zero or four. Form the 50 by 35
binary point-tile incidence matrix. Any possible reversal set is in
its kernel, since both weights are even. Its rank is 29. Among all
64 kernel vectors only zero meets every point zero or four times.
full_count.py checks these 64 vectors directly. Consequently the
space Y of classes over the identity base map has size 480^50 / 2^35.
Every graph automorphism acts faithfully on the base. Thus its full
group is its stabilizer in the 50-element cover group C5 x D10.

Counting fixed classes

The cover group acts on Y. For a cover automorphism g, a lift to the
480-state description permutes the seven ports at each point and adds
sign changes. On a tile those signs can be obtained by solving for
the switches that take its transported signed edge matrix to the
reference matrix of the target tile. Such switches exist because the
two local orientations of each K5 edge differ at most by reversal.
All lifts differ by tile reversals. Freeness gives

 |Fix_Y(g)| = (1/2^35) sum over its 2^35 lifts h of |Fix(h)|.

Indeed, over each fixed Y-class there are exactly 2^35 representatives,
each fixed by exactly one lift. For other classes there are none.

Here are the five contributions. The two groups of order-five elements
distinguish the two short-tile stabilizer directions from the other four
directions in the translation plane.

   order of g    number     |Fix_Y(g)|
       1            1       2^215 * 15^50
       2            5       2^121 * 15^20 * 61^2
       5            8       2^39 * 15^10
       5           16       2^43 * 15^10
      10           20       2^25 * 15^4

full_count.py evaluates that sum without listing all individual lifts.
Along an orbit of tiles, conjugation by tile reversals moves all sign
changes to one chosen representative, leaving only their parity.
If there are c tile orbits, each parity vector represents 2^(35-c)
lifts. A lift fixing a tuple has h^d=1 for d the order of g, since
h^d is a tile reversal and the action is free. This determines the
required parity on an orbit of length L whenever d/L is odd. When
d/L is even the d-th power of the reference lift on that orbit must
already be trivial. The remaining parity is free. For an admissible
lift, one local state can be selected per orbit of points, subject
to its return map fixing that state. The program tests all 480 states
under each distinct signed permutation of seven ports. Multiplying
around the point orbits gives |Fix(h)|. Averaging the table above
over the cover group yields the displayed result.

Independent count by subgroups

stabilizers.py generates all 20 subgroups of the cover group. For each
it first takes its odd-order translation subgroup O. A Y-class fixed
by O has an O-invariant representative. In detail, the local linear
codes must repeat on O-orbits. Write the sign choices modulo these
codes as an affine binary space with the induced permutations of O.
The sum of the O-translates of one sign choice differs from it by
tile reversals, since |O| is odd. This gives the invariant representative.
Two invariant representatives in a single class differ by an invariant
tile reversal, by freeness. Thus on the quotient one can select one
of 480 states per point orbit and divide by two per tile orbit.

If the subgroup contains an involution, it acts on these quotient
orbits. The second program derives an involutive lift geometrically:
at a flag remember the four-edge star containing the head of the
corresponding oriented K5 edge. Transporting that star determines
whether its orientation is reversed. Each transposed pair of point
orbits contributes 480 choices. At a fixed orbit the checker solves
(I+P)z=s modulo each invariant simplex code for its port permutation
P and signs s. This gives the same local counts by linear algebra.
Only the fixed tile orbits require a sum over the choice of a reversal.
The two flips on a transposed pair must agree. This computes fixed
classes for every subgroup, including the noncyclic ones.

Subtracting exact stabilizers of larger subgroups and dividing by orbit
size gives the distribution in stabilizers.out. Its total agrees with
the element-wise calculation. Just 657876422586110467860534224299019607046352151571854721293271488
of the unlabeled graphs have a nontrivial automorphism, a fraction
of approximately 9.7972 x 10^-60. The only possible full group orders
are 1, 2, 5, 10, 25 and 50, and all six occur. The groups of orders
25 and 50 have independently been computed on every ordinary graph in
the translation-invariant catalogue.
