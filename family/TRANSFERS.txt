Transferring the 400 construction to other parameters
24 November 2026

A direct extension using any one signed Johnson graph as the tile
reaches only the 400 example among the rows marked open in the local
Brouwer table through order 1,300. Mixing two types of tile gives a
specific route to two open rows on 616 vertices. We have suitable
signed matrices and individually valid translation orbits. The joint
edge decomposition is unresolved.

1. Signed Johnson graphs from the boundary of a simplex

Index the columns of D by the j-subsets of an m-set and its rows by
the (j-1)-subsets. Use the oriented simplicial boundary matrix. Then

                          X = jI - D^T D

has entries 0,+1,-1, diagonal zero and support the Johnson graph
J(m,j). Its degree is j(m-j). On exterior powers, D is contraction
by the all-one vector and its adjoint is exterior multiplication by
that vector. Their anticommutator is mI and their squares are zero.
Thus (D^T D)^2 = m D^T D and the eigenvalues of X are j and j-m.
Either X or -X can be used. The original oriented K5 incidence
construction is the case (m,j)=(5,2).

Take h vertices per fiber and use the h-1 balanced columns of a
normalized real Hadamard matrix. Each used base pair has a half-block.
All unused pairs have zero blocks. The fibers are either all empty or
all complete, indicated by c=0 or 1. Taking the complement also covers
the case where every unused pair has a full block. Let a=h/2, let t
be the number of base points and r>s the desired restricted eigenvalues.

On tile contrasts the action is -cI plus or minus aX. With
u=(r+c)/a and v=-(s+c)/a, both positive integers, one must have
m=u+v, tile size binomial(u+v,u), and tile degree uv. Every base point
lies in h-1 tiles. Thus the base degree b and graph degree satisfy

                 b=(h-1)uv,       k=c(h-1)+ab.

The tile size divides t(h-1) and cannot exceed t. The quotient on the
fiber constants is c(h-1)I+aB, where B is the base graph. If B is
noncomplete its required restricted eigenvalues p,q determine the
base parameters by mu_B=b+pq and lambda_B=mu_B+p+q. transfers.py
checks all possible real Hadamard orders (2 or a multiple of 4) as
an arithmetic superset, both signs, both interiors, and both row
orientations in current_table.csv. These are restrictions on this
one-tile-type construction. Scaled full/empty mixtures, mixed tile
types and other local frames have a different arithmetic.

The candidates left by the divisibility checks are in transfers.out.
Their fates can be decided without a packing search.

* At 288 and 1200 one needs a 2-(q^2,q,1) design on the complete
  base graph, with q=6 and q=10 respectively. Such a design is an
  affine plane and completes to a projective plane. Order 6 fails the
  Bruck–Ryser condition and order 10 is excluded by Lam, Thiel and
  Swiercz. https://doi.org/10.4153/CJM-1949-009-2
  https://www.cecm.sfu.ca/organics/papers/lam/
* At 456 the required base has complement (57,14,1,4), excluded by
  Wilbrink and Brouwer. At 624 the required base complement would have
  lambda=-4. https://aeb.win.tue.nl/graphs/srg/srgtab51-100.html
* At 736 the complete base requires 46 blocks of size 14 on 92 points,
  covering every pair once. Fisher's inequality requires at least 92
  blocks. At 960 the base is the complement of the unique triangular
  graph T(16). A clique is a matching on 16 objects, of size at most 8.
  A tile needs 14. https://aeb.win.tue.nl/graphs/srg/srgtab101-150.html
* At 400 the three original starters give the required cover.

2. A mixed construction for (616,287,126,140)

Use the complete 11-partite graph B with seven points per part. Its
degree is 70 and its restricted eigenvalues are 0 and -7. Replace each
point by a complete K8, using the seven binary characters as before.
The quotient action 7I+4B has eigenvalues 287, 7, -21. On a signed
tile the action is -I+4X, so the required signed eigenvalues are
2 and -5. Both tile types below are 10-regular.

For the first tile use the 21 edges of K7, with X=2I-D^T D from its
oriented incidence matrix. For the second, take the 28 doubled E8
roots R in roots616.txt. The certificate is R^T R=28I and all diagonal
entries of RR^T are 8. The off-diagonal entries are in {0,+4,-4}.
Thus X=2I-RR^T/4 has the required spectrum. The roots R/2 generate
all of E8, as witnessed by the Hermite normal form of R^T, of
determinant 256. The coordinates were found by selecting root-line
orbits under (012)(345) and imposing the eight-dimensional frame
operator. frames616.py checks the submitted coordinates directly.

Represent base points by (g,y) with g in Z/11 indexing the parts and y
in {0,...,6}. Translate the first coordinate. One orbit of each tile
uses 11(21+28)=539 flags, precisely 77 x 7. The two starters contain
105+140=245 edges. To complete the construction, assign first and
second coordinates to their vertices so their base edges represent
all 245 translation classes exactly once. This entails injectivity
within each tile, and distinct parts at the endpoints of every tile
edge. Vertices in different tiles may share a base label.

For colors y<z record the nonzero directed first-coordinate difference
from y to z. For equal colors record the nonzero difference up to sign.
Each of the 21 different-color pairs needs ten differences and each of
the seven equal-color pairs needs five. These requirements are both
necessary and sufficient for the two C11 orbits to give the whole
decomposition. The seven incidences at each point then follow from
degree 70 and local tile degree ten.

colors616.txt gives a coloring of the 49 starter entries satisfying
all 28 of these quotas. The first 21 entries follow lexicographic order
on the edges of K7, and the last 28 follow the order of roots616.txt.
positions616.txt gives first coordinates for which each tile orbit is
individually collision-free, covering 1,155 and 1,540 base edges.
Even after the best nonzero scalar rescaling of one of these particular
starters, there are 42 overlapping difference classes. A joint solve
with both sets of positions free and these colors fixed reached its
time limit. This gives neither a full cover nor an infeasibility result.

3. Why the same cover would also give (616,300,152,140)

For the supplied coloring the K7 triangle on nodes 0,1,3 uses edges
01,03,13 with distinct colors 5,0,6. The oriented three-edge cycle
with coefficients +1,-1,+1 is in the kernel of D. Thus its signed
three-term vector has X-eigenvalue 2. This triangle condition also
suffices for any other valid C11 coloring with a rainbow K7 triangle.

Suppose the two starter orbits give the full cover. Take the cycle
vector in each translate of the triangular tile and realize it in the
corresponding character spaces of the graph. Use the base signing
2I-D^T D, transported throughout the orbit. The eleven translates use
33 different base points, because the three colors are distinct.
Each part contains exactly three such points. On those fibers the
resulting function takes only values +1 and -1 and has mean zero.
On the remaining four fibers in each part, put the constant +1 on
two fibers and -1 on two others. This part of the function lies in
the zero eigenspace of B. The sum is a sign vector w on all 616
vertices. It has A-eigenvalue 7 on both the contrast and quotient
spaces and has coordinate sum zero.

The Seidel matrix S=J-I-2A has eigenvalues -15 and 41, with S w=-15w.
Switch by diag(w). The switched Seidel matrix has constant row sum
-15. Its graph has degree 315, restricted eigenvalues 7,-21, and
parameters (616,315,154,168). Complementation gives (616,300,152,140).
Both this row and the degree-287 row are marked open in Brouwer's table.
https://aeb.win.tue.nl/graphs/srg/srgtab601-650.html

Reproduce the positive local certificates and the bounded joint test
with the following commands from this directory.

    python transfers.py
    python frames616.py
    python transfer616.py
    python join616.py --seconds 25

The first three require no solver. The last uses OR-Tools CP-SAT.
